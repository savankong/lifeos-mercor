## Overview

Co-founder of Mercor; served as COO before transitioning to **Chairman in October 2025**. One of the three Bellarmine College Preparatory debate-team friends who founded the company with Brendan Foody and Adarsh Hiremath in January 2023; Thiel Fellow. Youngest of the three billionaire co-founders (by ~2 months) as of October 2025. *(HIGH — Wikipedia, verified via fetch)*
