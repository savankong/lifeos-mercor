## Overview

Co-founder and CEO of Handshake, the university career-services platform founded in 2014 out of Michigan Tech. In early 2024, decided to stand up a new internal AI-training-data business unit (Handshake AI) inside the existing company rather than spin out a new startup — a "re-founding" that leveraged Handshake's existing student/employer network to sell expert human feedback to frontier labs starting January 2025. By late 2024 he reportedly projected the AI unit would overtake Handshake's decade-old core recruiting business. *(HIGH for the Wikipedia-sourced background, verified via fetch; MED for the strategy narrative/quotes, sourced from a Lenny's Newsletter interview and AI-native GTM piece found via search but not independently fetched)*
