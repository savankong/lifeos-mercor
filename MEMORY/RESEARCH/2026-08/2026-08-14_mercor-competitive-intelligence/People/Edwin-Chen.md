## Overview

Founder and CEO of Surge AI (founded 2020). Ex-Google, ex-Twitter; MIT background. Built Surge to ~$1.2–1.4B revenue with only ~110–121 employees, entirely bootstrapped with **zero venture capital** through at least mid-2025 — an unusual position in a market where every other major competitor (Mercor, Micro1, Handshake, Invisible) is VC-backed. Reportedly began fundraising talks in July 2025 with a16z, Warburg Pincus, and TPG at valuations of $15–30B, while retaining ~75% personal ownership. *(HIGH for the Wikipedia-sourced facts, verified via fetch; MED for the specific fundraising-talks/valuation figures, sourced from aggregator pieces found via search but not independently fetched)*

## Relevance to Mercor

Chen is the one major founder in this space who has never needed to answer to venture investors, which is directly relevant to the "neutrality" dynamic that damaged Scale AI post-Meta-deal — Surge's independence is a competitive asset Mercor cannot claim once (if) it takes a strategic investor.
