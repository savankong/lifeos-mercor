## Overview

Co-founder and CEO of Mercor. One of three Bay Area high-school debate-team friends (Bellarmine College Preparatory) who founded the company in January 2023; dropped out of college and was awarded a Thiel Fellowship. Became one of the world's three youngest self-made billionaires (age 22) when Mercor hit its $10B Series C valuation in October 2025. *(HIGH — Wikipedia, Forbes/Fortune coverage via search, corroborated across multiple outlets)*

## Notable / Risk Flags

- Personally angel-invested in **Deeptune's $43M Series A** (~April 2026) before having Mercor acquire Deeptune three months later (July 2026). Told Fortune the investment was "in a lot of ways the main motivation" for backing Deeptune early — a self-dealing / insider-positioning flag, not an allegation of wrongdoing. *(HIGH — Fortune, verified via fetch)*
- Announced (per TechCrunch, Jul 2026) that Mercor's annualized run rate crossed $2B, +100% in four months, while the company was also managing fallout from the March 2026 data breach.
