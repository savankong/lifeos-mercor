## Overview

Co-founder and CTO of Mercor. One of the three Bellarmine College Preparatory debate-team friends who founded the company in January 2023 with Brendan Foody and Surya Midha; Thiel Fellow, dropped out of college. Became one of the world's youngest self-made billionaires alongside his co-founders at age 22 (October 2025, $10B valuation). *(HIGH — Wikipedia, Forbes profile page found via search)*
