\# Your Roster Pivot: High-Net-Worth Former Government Officials

**Status:** CONFIRMED DIRECTION (founder decision, 2026-08-16) — not a hypothesis under evaluation
**Pass 14**

## The pivot, stated plainly

Your Roster is repositioning around a specific asset: access to a network of senior, high-net-worth former government officials, and monetizing the institutional knowledge that population holds (policy formation, agency decision-making, program/budget processes, interagency coordination) by supplying it to AI data companies and, through them, frontier labs. This supersedes the earlier, broader "defense/government expertise" framing from Passes 5 and 11, which was this vault's own illustrative example, not evidence of a real asset. **The founder has confirmed this network access is real and is proceeding on that basis.** This document records the pivot and its first concrete action, not a re-litigation of whether the underlying assumption holds — that determination belongs to the founder, not to this vault's research.

## First concrete action

An outreach email to a personal contact at **Innodata** (see `Companies/Innodata.md`, `YOUR-ROSTER-BROKER-ANGLES.md`) proposing a supply relationship: Your Roster's government-official network feeding into Innodata's existing **SME-on-Demand program** and/or its **security-cleared federal teams** infrastructure — the one channel identified anywhere in this vault's research with a plausible existing pathway for this kind of expertise (`YOUR-ROSTER-GOVERNMENT-DEMAND-ANALYSIS.md` found no equivalent at Mercor, Scale AI, Surge AI, or Handshake AI). Full email text held outside the vault (sent directly, not a vault artifact) — outreach was formal, led with the network/expertise claim directly, and proposed a call to identify Innodata's specific capacity gaps rather than presuming them.

## What stays true regardless of the pivot

The structural findings from Passes 10–12 don't change just because the target vertical narrowed to a specific network claim:

- **Ask what the buyer can't source before pitching what you have** — still the single highest-leverage move, and still unconfirmed whether Innodata (or anyone) has unmet demand specifically for this population.
- **The buyers can be structurally also the competitors** (`YOUR-ROSTER-DATA-BUSINESS-ASSESSMENT.md`, obstacle #1) — worth watching for in the Innodata conversation specifically, since Innodata already has SME-on-Demand infrastructure and cleared teams; the same "why buy access instead of recruiting directly" tension applies.
- **Quality/trust bar is high and rising in anything government/defense-adjacent** — the live Pentagon/Anthropic conflict and the Surge/Mercor China-data-exposure controversy (`YOUR-ROSTER-GOVERNMENT-DEMAND-ANALYSIS.md`, findings 1 and 4) mean provenance and vetting will likely come up early in any real conversation, not as a late-stage compliance formality.
