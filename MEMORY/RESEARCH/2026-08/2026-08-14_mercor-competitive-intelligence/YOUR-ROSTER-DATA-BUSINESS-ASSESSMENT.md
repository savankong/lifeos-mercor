\# Assessment: Can Your Roster Compete as a Data Seller?

**Status:** ASSESSMENT (analysis, not new fact-finding — synthesizes this vault's existing verified research)
**Pass 10 — 2026-08-16**
**The question asked:** users answer questions on Your Roster's site, Your Roster aggregates and sells the answers to Mercor, Handshake, or directly to Anthropic/OpenAI. Is this viable? This document is a direct assessment, not a build plan — see `YOUR-ROSTER-EXPERT-DATA-STRATEGY.md` for the phased implementation approach if the direction below is pursued.

## The model is proven — that's not the question

Users (professionals) answering or evaluating domain questions, aggregated and sold to AI labs as RLHF/SFT/eval data, is not speculative. It's already produced a $10B company (Mercor), a $1.4B-revenue bootstrapped company (Surge AI), and a business line that grew from $0 to a $1B+ run-rate in 15 months (Handshake AI) — see `LANDSCAPE.md` and `Products/Mercor-Post-Training-Suite.md`. The real question isn't whether the model works. It's whether a new entrant can win a piece of it.

## Four structural obstacles — not sales/execution problems, but the actual shape of the market

1. **The buyers already build this themselves, at scale, better.** Mercor's core moat is its AI-vetting engine matching experts to lab-specific tasks with a live feedback loop (`Products/Mercor-AI-Vetting-Assessment.md`). A batch of pre-collected Q&A from an unproven new source is a weaker version of what these companies already run. There's no obvious reason for Mercor, Surge, or Handshake to buy a finished dataset instead of just recruiting the same contributors directly into their own marketplace — which means **the intended buyers are structurally also the competitors**, not neutral customers.

2. **Frontier labs are unusually cautious about new data vendors right now, for a specific, documented reason.** The Scale AI collapse (`Trends/Post-Scale-Meta-Neutrality-Shift.md`) — Meta buying 49% of Scale, Google/OpenAI/xAI pulling contracts within weeks — was a *trust* failure, not a quality failure. Labs are now hyper-sensitive about chain-of-custody on anything touching training data; Mercor's own March 2026 breach (`Trends/AI-Data-Vendor-Security-Trust-Risk.md`) reinforced exactly that sensitivity. No public vendor-intake program was found for either Anthropic or OpenAI in this vault's research (unlike Mercor/Scale/Handshake, which all publish one) — a cold pitch from a new, unproven source would be a long, relationship-driven enterprise sale, not a form-fill.

3. **Quality infrastructure is expensive before it's profitable.** Verified rates in this market run **$85–400+/hr** for credentialed specialists (`LANDSCAPE.md`'s talent-segmentation table). Contributor pay, rubric/QA design, and calibration infrastructure all have to be built and funded *before* any buyer commits — with no guarantee one does.

4. **No identified wedge yet.** The one thing that would make a buyer prefer Your Roster over building it themselves is access to a professional population they can't otherwise reach at comparable depth. Nothing in this vault's research currently establishes that — "professionals with 5+ years experience and 500+ connections" is not a niche Mercor doesn't already own. A real wedge would need to come from Your Roster's actual signup data (an unexpectedly deep vertical, a demographic or industry concentration competitors aren't already saturating), not be assumed from the current warm-intro product.

## Bottom line

Good model, hard entry. Without a specific, defensible niche, this is a capital- and trust-intensive race against companies with a 2–3 year head start and, in Mercor's case, ~$492M raised. The lower-risk way to get exposure to this market's economics remains the referral path already laid out in `YOUR-ROSTER-EXPERT-DATA-STRATEGY.md` (Path A) — pointing users at Mercor/Surge/Micro1/Handshake's own expert programs and monetizing the placement, not the data. That doesn't preclude testing the direct-data-sale idea later, once real usage data reveals whether an actual wedge exists.
