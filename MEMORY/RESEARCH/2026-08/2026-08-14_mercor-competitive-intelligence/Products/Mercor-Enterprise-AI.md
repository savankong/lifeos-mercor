\# Mercor Enterprise AI

**Status:** RESEARCHED
**Category:** Product
**Value:** HIGH

## Overview

A Forward-Deployed-Engineer-style AI agent consulting product, distinct from Mercor's original expert-marketplace/RLHF business. Structured as a four-phase engagement:

1. **Discover (Agent Diagnostics)** — workflow mapping and organizational analysis to identify high-value AI opportunities, using "AI interviews" to capture tacit process knowledge. Deliverables: ranked opportunities, workflow maps, implementation roadmaps, ROI projections.
2. **Deploy (Agent Deployment)** — builds and integrates production AI agents into existing enterprise systems, with custom evaluations aligned to organizational standards.
3. **Improve (Agent Optimization)** — ongoing architecture review, prompt refinement, tool optimization, model-selection improvements, continuous performance monitoring.
4. **Monetize (Data Monetization)** — the on-ramp into the separate Mercor Data product (see `Mercor-Data-Monetization.md`): enterprises license their anonymized operational data to AI labs.

*(HIGH — verified via direct fetch of mercor.com/enterprise/)*

## Target Customers & GTM

Organizations with 1,000+ employees, private-equity firms doing AI-related due diligence, and specific functions (SRE/software engineering, finance/operations, customer support). No public pricing — prospects are routed to "book a demo," indicating a conventional high-touch enterprise sales cycle, in contrast to the founder-led, no-sales-team motion Mercor has described for its core marketplace. Security posture emphasized: agents operate only within explicitly authorized systems/data, flexible managed-vs-customer-controlled deployment, full auditability. *(HIGH — verified via fetch)*
