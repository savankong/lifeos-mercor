\# Deeptune Agent-Training Environments (acquired by Mercor, Jul 2026)

**Status:** RESEARCHED
**Category:** Product
**Value:** HIGH

## Overview

Deeptune builds simulation environments where AI agents practice real-world software tasks (e.g., Excel, Salesforce, Slack) before deployment — described by Fortune as comparable to a flight simulator for AI agents. Mercor acquired Deeptune in July 2026 (undisclosed terms); the Deeptune team relocated to Mercor's New York office. The deal pairs Mercor's expert task-grading network with Deeptune's agent-training-environment tech, positioning Mercor to sell "agentic" training data/evals as a new product line beyond its existing RLHF/SFT/rubrics suite. *(HIGH — Fortune, verified via fetch)*
