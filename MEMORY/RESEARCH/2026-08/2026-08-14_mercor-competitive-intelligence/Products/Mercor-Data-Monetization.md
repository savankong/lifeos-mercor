\# Mercor Data (Enterprise Workflow Data)

**Status:** RESEARCHED
**Category:** Product
**Value:** HIGH

## Overview

Lets enterprises anonymize and license their internal operational data to AI labs in exchange for compensation, with Mercor acting as the intermediary handling extraction, anonymization, lab-matching, and licensing. Positioned as giving AI labs access to "how organizations actually operate" — data unavailable through public datasets or Mercor's expert-marketplace product. *(HIGH — verified via direct fetch of mercor.com/data/)*

## Data Categories Captured (via 50+ integrations, OAuth-based)

1. Messaging & communication — messages, emails, meeting transcripts, video calls
2. Documents & collaboration — document edits, wikis, cloud storage
3. Project management — task completions, calendar events, thread activity
4. CRM & sales data
5. Code & repositories — engineering workflows
6. Finance & HR — financial and HR operations data

## Value Proposition & Incentives

- **For enterprises (sellers):** compensation for data contribution, referral bonuses of **up to $100K**, a custom AI analysis of their own operations as a byproduct, no upfront cost.
- **For AI labs (buyers):** authentic enterprise operational data at scale, rather than public/synthetic data.

## Security & Compliance Posture (as claimed by Mercor)

- SOC 2 Type II certified
- AWS KMS encryption
- Proprietary anonymization claimed to detect **60+ types of sensitive identifiers**
- "Fully automated extraction with zero manual data review by external parties"
- Complete audit logging, scoped access, credential isolation

*(HIGH that these are Mercor's claims, verified via direct fetch; MED on independent verification of the security claims themselves — no third-party audit or breach-history-adjusted assessment found. Given Mercor's own March 2026 breach (see `../Companies/Mercor.md`), these security claims deserve more scrutiny than face value in any diligence process.)*

## Relevance

This is arguably the most sensitive product in Mercor's portfolio from a customer-trust standpoint — it explicitly asks enterprises to pipe internal Slack/email/CRM/code data through Mercor to third-party AI labs. It sits directly downstream of the "Monetize" phase of Mercor Enterprise AI (see `Mercor-Enterprise-AI.md`) and is a new, higher-risk/higher-reward vector beyond the credentialed-expert-labor model that built Mercor's reputation.
