\# Mercor Post-Training Data Suite

**Status:** RESEARCHED
**Category:** Product
**Value:** CRITICAL

## Overview

Mercor's core current revenue product: a set of offerings sold to frontier AI labs covering the post-training data lifecycle, per Contrary Research (verified via fetch):

- **RLHF** — domain experts provide preference rankings comparing model outputs
- **SFT** — creation of gold-standard prompt/response pairs
- **Rubrics** — scoring frameworks decomposing quality judgments into measurable criteria
- **RL Environments** — simulated work settings where AI agents execute multi-step tasks
- **APEX Benchmarks** — evaluation frameworks measuring AI performance on "economically valuable tasks" across investment banking, consulting, law, and medicine

This is the direct product-level battleground versus Surge AI (RLHF/red-teaming breadth), Turing (coding-specific data), Handshake AI (STEM expert feedback), and Micro1 (similar full-stack expert marketplace).

## Update (iteration 3, Aug 2026): APEX has become a benchmark family

Live site structure at mercor.com (verified via direct fetch) shows APEX has expanded from a single benchmark into branded sub-leaderboards: **APEX** (flagship "AI Productivity Index"), **APEX-Agents** (agent performance), **APEX-Accounting**, and **APEX-SWE** (software engineering). This serves a dual purpose beyond pure data-collection: it's also a **public marketing/credibility asset** — labs and enterprises can point to APEX leaderboard placement as a neutral-seeming quality signal, which reinforces Mercor's brand as an evaluation authority, not just a labor vendor. *(HIGH — verified via fetch)*
