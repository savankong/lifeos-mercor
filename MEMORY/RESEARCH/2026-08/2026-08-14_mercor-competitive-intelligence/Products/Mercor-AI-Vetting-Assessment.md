\# Mercor AI Vetting / Assessment Engine ("Mercor Voice")

**Status:** RESEARCHED
**Category:** Product
**Value:** HIGH

## Overview

Mercor's original product line, dating to its 2023 founding as an AI-powered technical-recruiting marketplace: an AI system that conducts ~20-minute video interviews and analyzes candidate profiles across a claimed pool of 300,000+ professionals, screening down to a smaller vetted pool. As of the Series C period, Mercor reported having evaluated **468,000+ applicants** and maintaining **30,000+ active expert contractors** across 45+ countries. This vetting engine is the underlying "supply-side" infrastructure that now feeds the expert-data business, not a separate legacy product. *(HIGH — Sacra and Contrary Research, both verified via fetch)*

**Iteration-3 update:** this same underlying interview/vetting AI is referenced elsewhere (search results, not the primary site fetch) under the product name **"Mercor Voice."** The exact current branding boundary — whether "Mercor Voice" is a formally distinct product name or just descriptive shorthand used in commentary — was not confirmed against a canonical Mercor source in this pass. *(MED — treat "Mercor Voice" as a plausible but unconfirmed product name for this same engine)*

## Relevance

This is Mercor's actual defensibility claim: the vetting/matching data flywheel (interview + performance data improving future matching) is what the company points to as differentiation versus competitors who source experts through less rigorous or less proprietary screening. Worth pressure-testing in a later iteration — vetting quality claims are marketing-friendly and hard to verify externally.
