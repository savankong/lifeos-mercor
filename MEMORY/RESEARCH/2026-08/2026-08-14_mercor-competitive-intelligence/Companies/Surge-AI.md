\# Surge AI

**Status:** RESEARCHED

## Overview

Surge AI (Surge HQ) is a San Francisco RLHF and data-annotation company founded in **2020 by Edwin Chen** (ex-Google, Twitter, MIT), built in direct reaction to what he saw as poor quality in existing crowdsourced-labeling platforms. Surge coordinates roughly **1 million annotators** and focuses on RLHF, red-teaming/adversarial testing, and RL environments — breadth across nearly every major AI lab rather than Mercor's narrower credentialed-expert niche. *(HIGH — Wikipedia, verified via fetch)*

## Scale & Financials

- Revenue: **$1.2B (2024)**, reported at **$1.4B ARR** in 2026 reporting.
- Employees: only ~110–121 — extraordinarily capital-efficient (~$9.9M revenue per employee cited in one estimate).
- **Fully bootstrapped, zero VC funding**, through at least June 2025 — the outlier in this market, where every other major player has raised large venture rounds.
- Clients reportedly include Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Meta, and the U.S. Army.
- By July 2025, founder Edwin Chen was reportedly in talks with a16z, Warburg Pincus, and TPG for a raise; reported terms ranged from a **$1B Series A at $24–30B valuation**, with Chen retaining ~75% ownership. Status of that raise as of Aug 2026 not independently confirmed in this pass — **flagged for iteration 2 verification**.

## Relevance to Mercor

Surge is the closest thing to a #1 incumbent-successor after the Scale/Meta split — it reportedly "took the frontier-lab crown" post-Scale. It competes with Mercor for the same reallocated lab budgets but via a different model: Surge's edge is annotator scale + bootstrapped credibility/independence (no VC or strategic investor to create a neutrality conflict); Mercor's edge is credentialed domain-expert depth (PhDs, doctors, lawyers) for harder reasoning/professional-domain tasks.
