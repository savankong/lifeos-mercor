## Overview

Centaur Labs (Boston, founded 2017) is a medical-data-annotation company that is **healthcare-only**, unlike Mercor/Surge/Scale/Handshake's cross-domain model. Uses a global network of **58,000+ healthcare experts and medical students** to label multimodal clinical data — imaging (X-ray, CT/MRI, pathology), video (surgical footage), text (clinical notes), audio (heart/lung sounds), and waveforms — for AI model training and evaluation. Runs a gamified consumer-facing app, **DiagnosUs**, with pay-for-performance incentives. *(HIGH — verified via search of company materials and third-party coverage)*

## Credibility Signal

Peer-reviewed research from **Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center** and **Mass General Brigham** reportedly found Centaur Labs' crowdsourced expert labelers **outperform board-certified physicians on labeling accuracy** — a notable third-party validation most competitors in this vault don't have. *(MED — claim found via multiple search results describing the studies, not independently verified by fetching the papers themselves)*
