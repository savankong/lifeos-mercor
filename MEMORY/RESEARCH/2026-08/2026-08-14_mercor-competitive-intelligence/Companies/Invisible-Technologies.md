## Overview

Invisible Technologies, founded in **2015**, is an older human-in-the-loop / AI-automation company that operates at the intersection of AI automation and human-in-the-loop work, distinct from the newer expert-marketplace startups. For AI training data, it handles the full pipeline: task decomposition, annotator sourcing/training, quality control, and delivery of labeled datasets. *(MED — found via search, single-source, not independently fetched)*

## Scale & Financials

- Total raised: **$144M** as of February 2026, including a **$100M round in September 2025**.
- Revenue: **~$134M in 2024** (per iteration-5 search finding) — this is revenue, distinct from the total-raised figure above; the two should not be conflated.
- Clients reportedly include Microsoft, Cohere, and AWS.
- A cofounder has publicly stated the company is committed to **staying independent** — explicit neutrality positioning, the same argument Mercor/Surge/Turing make against Scale AI post-Meta-deal.

It positions around **process/pipeline capability** (task decomposition, annotator training, QC, delivery) rather than around *who* the annotators are. *(MED — inference from the absence of a talent-focused positioning in available sources, not a confirmed statement from the company — flagged for verification in a future pass)*
