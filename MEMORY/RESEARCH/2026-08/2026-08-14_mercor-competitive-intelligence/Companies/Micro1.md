\# Micro1

**Status:** RESEARCHED
**Category:** Company — Core Competitor
**Value:** HIGH

## Overview

Micro1 is a vertically-integrated expert-labor marketplace operating a business model very close to Mercor's: it sources vetted domain experts (PhDs in medicine, law, physics; senior software engineers) for AI labs' RLHF/model-training needs, marking up expert labor while providing AI-powered vetting, workflow management, QA, and global payroll compliance. It accepts roughly the top 1% of applicants and uses proprietary AI interviewing to screen thousands of candidates daily, with hiring turnaround under 48 hours. It is also expanding into robotics training data and a broader AI-recruiting product called **Zara**. *(HIGH — Sacra, verified via fetch)*

## Scale & Financials

- Revenue: crossed **$100M ARR in December 2025** (from $7M at the start of 2025); reached **~$300M annualized revenue by April 2026**.
- Valuation: **$500M** as of a $35M Series A led by **01 Advisors** (Sept 2025).

*(HIGH — Sacra, verified via direct fetch)*

## Relevance to Mercor

Micro1 is Mercor's closest structural analog among the smaller competitors — same expert-marketplace mechanics, similar AI-vetting pitch, similar customer set (frontier labs post-Scale-exodus) — but at roughly 1/7th Mercor's revenue scale and a fraction of its valuation. Worth tracking as a potential acquisition target or as the marketplace model's #2 proof point (i.e., is Mercor's growth company-specific or is the whole expert-marketplace category growing this fast).
