\# The Post-Scale/Meta "Neutrality" Shift

**Status:** RESEARCHED
**Category:** Trend
**Value:** CRITICAL

## Overview

In **June 2025, Meta acquired a 49% stake in Scale AI for $14.3B** and brought Scale founder Alexandr Wang in-house to lead its superintelligence effort. Because frontier labs route highly sensitive unreleased-model data through their labeling vendor, a vendor now substantially owned by a direct AI-lab competitor (Meta) became commercially untenable for other labs. **Google, OpenAI, and xAI reportedly cut or reduced ties with Scale within weeks.** That reallocated spend is the single biggest external driver of growth across this entire competitive set in 2025–2026 — Surge AI, Mercor, Turing, Handshake, Invisible Technologies, Micro1, and Snorkel all cite or are reported to have benefited from it. *(HIGH — corroborated across Contrary Research (fetched) and multiple search-surfaced pieces (Teahose, Value Add VC))*

## Why it matters for Mercor specifically

Mercor's own reported ARR trajectory (~$75M Feb 2025 → ~$450M Oct 2025) tracks almost exactly the period after the Meta/Scale deal, per Contrary Research. This is the load-bearing fact behind Mercor's growth story: much of it is *market reallocation*, not pure category expansion, which has two implications worth flagging for whoever this research informs — (1) growth this fast, from a single external shock, may not be a repeatable pattern going forward, and (2) Mercor's own neutrality is now a strategic asset it needs to protect (see Deeptune conflict-of-interest note and the March 2026 breach — both are neutrality/trust risks of a different kind).
