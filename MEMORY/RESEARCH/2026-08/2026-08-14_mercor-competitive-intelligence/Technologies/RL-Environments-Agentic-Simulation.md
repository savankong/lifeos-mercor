## Overview

An emerging technical category adjacent to RLHF: simulated task environments (e.g., Deeptune's Excel/Salesforce/Slack simulations) where AI **agents** — not just chat models — practice multi-step, real-world software tasks under supervision, generating training/eval data for agentic AI. Mercor moved into this category via its July 2026 acquisition of Deeptune, positioning the company beyond pure human-feedback labeling and into agent-training infrastructure. This looks like the next frontier after RLHF/SFT saturate — worth tracking which competitors move here next (Turing's coding focus and Surge's RL-environments work already overlap this space). *(HIGH — Fortune, verified via fetch, for the Deeptune specifics; MED for the "next frontier" framing, which is this vault's own synthesis rather than a sourced claim)*
