**Status:** RESEARCHED
**Category:** Technology

## Overview

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) — and its adjacent techniques (SFT gold-pair creation, rubric-based grading, reasoning-trace generation) — is the core technical process the entire competitive set (Mercor, Scale AI, Surge AI, Turing, Handshake, Micro1, Invisible, Snorkel) is monetizing. The specific sub-niche that differentiates competitors is *whose* human judgment is being captured: general crowdworkers (older data-labeling model), broad annotator pools at scale (Surge's ~1M annotators), or credentialed domain experts — doctors, lawyers, PhDs, senior engineers — for harder professional-reasoning tasks (Mercor's and Micro1's specific angle). *(HIGH — synthesized from Contrary Research and Sacra, both verified via fetch)*
