\# AI-Powered Candidate Vetting / Interviewing

**Status:** RESEARCHED (light)
**Category:** Technology
**Value:** HIGH

## Overview

An AI system conducting automated interviews (Mercor: ~20-minute video interviews; Micro1: AI-driven technical interviews screening thousands/day) to rapidly filter large applicant pools down to a small vetted supply of experts, at speed and cost human recruiters can't match. This is the supply-side infrastructure underneath the expert-marketplace business model shared by Mercor and Micro1 in particular, and is presented by both companies as a core defensibility/moat claim. Not independently verified for accuracy/bias characteristics in this pass — vendor-reported vetting-quality claims should be treated skeptically until externally audited. *(MED — vendor-sourced claims via Sacra, verified as *stated*, not verified as *true*)*
